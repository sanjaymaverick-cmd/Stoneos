## Why this cannot post to current StoneOS
- `Slab.slabSerial` is unique per piece (`CONTEXT.md`: `{blockSerial}/{totalCut}/{sequence}`). A count-only lot has nowhere to live.
- `BillingMode` is `gst_invoice` or `cash_unbilled`, not both on one sale. A 50/50 split needs two legs on one commercial sale.
- `CashSale` is a separate document. It is not tied to a block quantity on the same invoice.
- Sale lines point at `slabId`. They cannot say VG-001 × 40.

Schema change that matches this run: one `block_lot` row with `good_count`, `broken_count`, `sold_count`; sale line is `(block_id, qty, taxable_amount, cash_amount)`; invoice posts only the taxable leg; cash note posts the cash leg; customer statement sums both.
