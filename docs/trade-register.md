# Local sales and raw purchase register

Books → Local sales & raw purchases records dated local non-GST sales and raw block purchases. It supports named cash/bank/UPI accounts, partial and later payments, customer advances already present in the ledger, customer payments directly settling a creditor, and vendor advances. Sale notes, quantities, rates and settlement notes are retained. Amounts are the agreed net material values; price/rounding differences must be explained in the bill note. No GST sale document is generated by this register.

For local credit sales, additional non-material customer dues are separate from sales revenue. Net vendor pass-through amounts need not appear in the local cash sale record. A same-day cash adjustment handles a gross receipt in one account and a non-material outflow from cash without treating the difference as granite revenue. Sales commissions remain an expense.

Raw purchases retain actual agreed block value, the supplier invoice taxable value and explicitly supplied tax amounts separately. The whole actual value plus recorded GST becomes supplier payable, not merely the invoice value. Payments settle that payable. Cash royalty and inward freight are capitalized in raw stock once. Blocks are received atomically with their dated purchase and inventory movements. A trade reference prevents legacy purchase reporting from counting those blocks again.

Amounts are validated in paise, dates in the factory calendar, factory ownership on all reads/writes. Write retries require the same data; reference uniqueness and serialized factory transactions prevent duplicate bills and overpayments. Cash-day locks are enforced by normal voucher posting. The existing party statements, trial balance, cash drawer, CEO sales totals and dated analytics include register postings. Funds-account balances are visible on the register page.

Historical sale records may precede reliable dispatch-lot information. Those quantities are explicitly marked **Dispatch lot allocation pending** and do not silently decrement stock or invent COGS. Allocation requires the source lots and completed production records; stock and lot-level profitability remain incomplete until that reconciliation. The register currently records immutable bills and additional payments; corrections require reviewed reversal/replacement work, not silent edits.
